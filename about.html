ABOUT THE MOVEMENT

MOHAMMED ABDULLAHI ABUBAKAR, SAN, Support Campaign 2027

MOHAMMED ABDULLAHI ABUBAKAR, SAN, Support Campaign 2027 is a grassroots political movement established to mobilize citizens across Bauchi State in support of the governorship aspiration of MOHAMMED ABDULLAHI ABUBAKAR, SAN,.

The campaign is built on integrity, transparency, inclusiveness and accountability with the aim of providing quality leadership and sustainable development across the twenty Local Government Areas of Bauchi State.

We believe that together we can build a peaceful, prosperous and economically viable Bauchi State where every citizen has equal opportunities to succeed.

Our Vision

To build a united, peaceful and prosperous Bauchi State where every citizen enjoys quality education, accessible healthcare, economic opportunities, security and good governance.

Our Mission

To mobilize, educate and empower the people of Bauchi State while promoting transparent leadership, youth development, women empowerment and sustainable socio-economic transformation.
