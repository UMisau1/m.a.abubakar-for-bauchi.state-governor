01
GREAT GREEN WALL PROGRAMME

Combating Desertification & Restoring Landscapes

The Great Green Wall Programme was initiated by the African Union to combat desertification and promote sustainable environmental development across the Sahel region.

Bauchi State participated in the programme through interventions implemented in four Local Government Areas: Ganjuwa, Jama’are, Katagum and Itas-Gadau.

01 Establishment of 33 hectares of orchards across participating communities.
02 Establishment of 20 hectares of wood lots in the participating Local Government Areas.
03 Drilling of six solar-powered and three welding-powered boreholes to support communities.
04 Employment of 27 Forest Guards to protect plantations and programme facilities.
05 Construction of a Skill Acquisition Centre at Chinade village for youths within the Great Green Wall corridor.
06 Provision of about 12,600 improved date-palm seedlings for distribution to the public.
07 Donation of two tractors with complete implements including plough, harrow, water bowser and grass mower.
08 Continued plantation maintenance and environmental restoration activities.
02
BRIDGING TRUST FUND PROJECT

Sustainable Livelihoods & Community Support

The Bridging Trust Fund Project was established to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities for communities around reserves and protected areas, while reducing pressure on natural resources.

01 ₦12 million was disbursed to 20 communities selected from Toro, Ningi and Ganjuwa Local Government Areas.
02 A Train-the-Trainer workshop was conducted for 20 women on twine-bag and liquid-soap production.
03 3,000 assorted tree seedlings were distributed to participating communities to support orchard development.
04 A Toyota Hilux vehicle was provided to support supervision and implementation of the programme.
05 300 desks and customized exercise books were distributed to schools serving beneficiary communities.
